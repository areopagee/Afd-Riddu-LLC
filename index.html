About AFD Riddu LLC

AFD Riddu LLC is a logistics and supply chain solutions company based in Spring Hill, Tennessee. We help businesses plan and coordinate the movement of goods, from transportation and distribution to sourcing and import and export coordination.

We are committed to reliable logistics and to the satisfaction of the businesses we serve.

Our mission

To give businesses dependable logistics and supply chain support that makes moving goods simpler and more predictable.

Our vision

To be a trusted logistics partner that connects businesses with the markets and partners they need.

Our values

Reliability

We do what we say and plan with care.

Professionalism

Courteous, organized, and prepared in every interaction.

Transparency

Honest information about what we can and cannot do.

Customer focus

Your operational goals shape the solution, not the other way round.
