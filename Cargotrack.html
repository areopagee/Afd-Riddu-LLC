Privacy Policy

CargoTrack · Effective date: November 5, 2026

In short: CargoTrack works fully offline. Your shipment information stays on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

1. Who we are

CargoTrack (the “App”) is developed and published by AFD Riddu LLC (“we”, “us”). The App is an offline shipment and delivery management tool.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any information. We do not receive, store on our servers, or have access to anything you enter in the App.

The App contains no user accounts, registration, sign-in, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or third-party tracking tools, and it makes no network requests.

3. Information you enter in the App

You may enter shipment records such as shipment IDs, sender and recipient names, origin and destination, carrier names, tracking references, descriptions, dates, notes and status updates. This information is stored only locally on your device in the App’s private storage and is never sent to us or to any third party. Because you may type personal names into these fields, please only enter information you are entitled to store.

4. Permissions

The App does not request any Android permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files, phone identifiers or the internet.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your information, because we do not have it. No data is shared with advertisers, analytics providers or other third parties.

6. Device backups

Android may include app data in device backups depending on your device and Google account settings. Such backups are handled by your device and operating system provider under their own terms; we do not access them. You can control backups in your device settings.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can remove all shipment records at any time using Settings → Data Management → Clear all data, delete individual shipments inside the App, or uninstall the App to remove all of its locally stored data.

8. Security

Your data is kept in the App’s private storage area, which is protected by the Android operating system’s app sandbox. Since the App does not transmit data, nothing is exposed in transit. Please keep your device protected with a screen lock and up-to-date software.

9. Children’s privacy

The App is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this Privacy Policy, we will publish the updated version at this page and update the effective date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App’s store listing.

11. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact AFD Riddu LLC at ayoubuii99@gmail.com.
