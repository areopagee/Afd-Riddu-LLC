HomeVault Privacy Policy

App: HomeVault: Home Manager (Android) · Effective date: November 8, 2026

Summary: HomeVault works entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal information. It has no account system, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not request any Android permissions.

This Privacy Policy explains how HomeVault: Home Manager (“HomeVault”, “the app”), provided by Afd Riddu Team (“we”, “us”), handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any information. We do not receive, store or have access to the data you enter into HomeVault.

2. Information you enter into the app

HomeVault lets you record household information such as appliance details (name, brand, model, serial number, purchase date and price), warranty details, rooms, maintenance tasks, repairs, expenses, and service provider details (for example a company name, phone number, email address, street address or website that you choose to type in). This information:

  • is saved only in the app’s private storage on your device;
  • is never sent to us or to any third party by the app;
  • is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics.

Contact details of service providers remain stored locally. The app never calls, emails or opens any contact on its own.

3. Permissions

HomeVault does not request or use any Android permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files, calendar or phone.

4. Internet access and third parties

The app does not connect to the internet. It contains no advertising networks, analytics or crash-reporting services, no social media tools and no third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect data. Because we do not collect or share data, no data is disclosed to third parties.

5. Data storage, backup and deletion

  • Storage: your data is stored locally on your device.
  • Device backup: if you have Android’s backup feature turned on, your device settings may include app data in a backup to your own account. That backup is managed by your device and your account provider, not by us. You can control it in your device settings.
  • Deleting your data: you can delete individual records inside the app, use Settings → Reset all application data to erase everything, or uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings. Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot recover it for you.

6. Security

Your data stays in the app’s private storage, which is protected by the Android operating system. No data leaves your device through the app. Please also protect your device with a screen lock, because anyone who can unlock your device may be able to open the app.

7. Children’s privacy

HomeVault is a general-purpose home organization tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. Depending on where you live, you may have privacy rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA; you can contact us about any privacy question at the address below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the app adds new features. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed. If a future version of the app handles data differently, we will update this policy and the app’s Google Play data safety information before that version is released.
